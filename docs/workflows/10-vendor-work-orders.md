# Vendor / Work Orders V1

## Flow

Work Package Identified → Vendor Assigned → Scope / Rate Agreed → Work Order Issued → In Progress → Completed → Measurement Pending if required → Payable Ready → Closed

## Scope

- Purchase is for materials and items bought by GDS.
- Vendor Work Orders are for outsourced execution scope and remain separate from Purchase supplier records.
- Site Execution tracks actual progress, dates, blockers, QC, rectification and handover.
- Vendor Work Orders track the vendor, agreed scope, commercial commitment, document and payable readiness.
- Lump Sum work uses the agreed commercial value and may move from Completed to Payable Ready when the payable amount is confirmed.
- Measurement Based work keeps detailed measurements external in Google Drive or Sheets and requires the measurement link and payable amount before Payable Ready.

## Deferred

- Vendor payments and Accounts processing are deferred.
- Vendor Master is deferred.
- Detailed measurement rows, taxes, deductions and integrations are deferred.
